Asia’s demand story is shifting from volume to wallet and distribution. Korea’s Q2 foreign tourism spend reached ₩6.169T (~$4.41B), +33.6% YoY, while Japan set a first-half inbound-spend record of ¥4.85T (~$32.8B), +1.3% despite arrivals slipping 2%. In both markets, the strategic question is no longer simply “how many visitors?” but who captures spend and where. Edaily · Kyodo
China inbound is rapidly dispersing beyond the gateways. European-origin summer orders rose 275% YoY, attraction tickets more than twentyfold, and inbound payment value 72.9% since June. Qinhuangdao foreign air arrivals quadrupled, Erenhot tripled and Luoyang doubled — evidence that improved visas, payments and transport are moving international demand into regional China. Xinhua
Short booking windows and long-stay intent are coexisting. In India, 72% of Independence Day bookings landed in the prior four days, while China’s cooling destinations saw three-night-plus stays reach 60% of homestay bookings. The common thread is not trip duration; it is contextual relevance — fast conversion for an obvious occasion and a clear reason to stay longer. Business Standard · 中国网旅游
AI travel competition has moved from inspiration to execution. Fliggy’s Feizhu Bangbang can perform more than ten trip tasks — including cancellation, upgrades, seat selection, transfers and invoicing — while Google Maps’ Ask Maps in Japan turns conversational prompts, reviews, route context and permitted Gmail bookings into plans. Discovery, servicing and transaction are collapsing into one interface. Sina Finance · Travel Voice

💡 Airbnb APAC Implications

Build two distinct conversion systems: last-minute occasions and planned long stays. India’s Independence Day pattern calls for origin-based CRM in the final four days, instant book and drivable group homes. China’s 13-day Mid-Autumn/National Day “leave hack” and Korea’s potential 11-day autumn break call for early supply activation, kitchens, laundry, group capacity and multi-night value. One generic seasonal campaign will miss both behaviors.
Compete for the neighborhood wallet, not just the bed. Korea’s inbound spending is up sharply but concentrated in Seoul, dermatology and the largest merchants; Singapore is using Grab vouchers to move visitors into precincts after arrival. Airbnb’s advantage is strongest when the stay becomes a distribution point for food, culture and small local merchants — supported by useful host guides and post-booking discovery.
Trust evidence must sit inside AI discovery. In Malaysia, more than half use AI for trip logistics, yet 60% still demand verified reviews and only 30% trust AI suggestions outright. Complete structured listing data, recent reviews, accurate bed and amenity details, transparent total price and host reliability are not separate from the AI strategy; they are the evidence layer that lets AI recommendations convert.
Taiwan requires immediate policy clarity. Proposed weekday incentives offer up to NT$2,000 (~$63) across two nights, but current reporting says foreign-platform bookings may not qualify. Public policy should establish whether Airbnb’s licensed inventory is eligible before commercial teams reference the program. If excluded, the impact is structural: government-funded demand would be redirected toward direct-booked lodging.

🏢 Competitor & Platform Watch

Yanolja grows volume while profitability bends. H1 revenue rose 14% to ₩502.3B (~$359M), and global transaction value rose 26.8% to ₩21.4T (~$15.3B), but operating result swung to a ₩15.2B (~$10.9M) loss. NOL World and AI-powered inbound discovery remain active growth bets. Korea Economic Daily
Agoda and STB deepen from marketing into destination infrastructure. Their new three-year partnership covers joint campaigns, data strategy, AI recommendations and industry capability building. Agoda is positioning itself as an operating partner to destinations, not merely a paid distribution channel. TTG Asia
MakeMyTrip adds government-backed experiences at a low entry price. More than 350 guided tours across 60-plus Indian cities became bookable from Aug 15, spanning heritage, food, spirituality, villages and artisan workshops, with prices from ₹500 (~$6). The competitive front is expanding from accommodation to local discovery and cross-sell. ETTravelWorld
Apartment hotels are targeting Airbnb’s core Japan use cases. Japanese developers are increasing residential-style hotels for inbound families and longer stays, combining kitchens and space with standardized operations and hotel trust. Airbnb must make its differentiation — varied whole homes, neighborhood character and host service — more explicit. The Japan Times

🇮🇳 India (भारत)

• Independence Day demand was highly spontaneous: 72% booked in the final four days, hotel bookings rose 21% WoW and bus bookings 44%.

• India expanded the e-Visa gateway network to 88 ports; 95% of applications are processed within 72 hours.

• MakeMyTrip’s 350-plus government-backed guided experiences turn in-destination activity discovery into a more direct OTA battleground.

Independence Day exposes a trip-type split. IHG bookings rose 16–20% YoY; Delhi bus bookings rose 53%. Gen Z represented 23% of bus and 32% of air bookings, 75% of flyers were solo, yet 94% of hotel stays were groups. Merchandising should separate solo mobility from group accommodation rather than treat the weekend as one segment. Business Standard
New e-Visa gateways can distribute inbound demand. Bhopal and Tirupati airports plus nine land ports take the network to 88 — 37 airports, 38 seaports and 13 land ports — across 172 eligible nationalities. These gateways need translated destination content and supply readiness, not just national acquisition. India Today
Abu Dhabi subsidizes hotel-based packages for Indian travelers. The pilot covers up to 20,000 visas, saving Dh285 ($77) per eligible traveler who books a return flight and at least three hotel nights through partners. Because alternative stays are excluded, Airbnb needs a whole-trip value response rather than nightly-rate parity. The National

🇰🇷 Korea (대한민국)

• H1 inbound reached a record 10.71M, +21.3% YoY; Q2 foreign tourism spend grew even faster at 33.6%.

• Regional dispersal is real: Gyeongbuk foreign visits rose 26.4%, spending 16%, and camping mentions 700%.

• Yanolja’s transaction value grew 26.8%, but H1 operating result swung to a ₩15.2B (~$10.9M) loss.

Inbound wallet growth outpaces arrival growth. Foreign tourism spend reached ₩6.169T (~$4.41B), +33.6% YoY, while the top 1% of merchants captured 66.9% of analyzed overseas-card sales. The opportunity is to distribute high-value demand into neighborhoods and smaller merchants. Edaily
Gyeongbuk shows what dispersal can look like. Foreign visitors rose 26.4% to 2.05M and total tourism spend increased 16% to ₩1.5488T (~$1.11B), even as domestic visitors fell 2.9%. Camping mentions jumped 700%; experience and activity trips led motivations at 23.1%. Aju Business Daily
The autumn booking race has already started. Combining Chuseok, Hangul Day and leave can produce up to 11 consecutive days. Consumer interest is shifting toward supermarket exploration, regional snacks and independent bookstores — practical neighborhood culture that fits longer, residential stays. Segye Ilbo

🇯🇵 Japan (日本)

• H1 inbound fell 2.0% as Chinese arrivals dropped 56.4%, yet spending rose 1.3% to a record ¥4.85T (~$32.8B).

• Domestic overnight participation slipped, but spend rose 2.5% to ¥8.3883T (~$56.7B): growth came from value per trip, not traveler volume.

• Weak yen and inflation are shortening trips: 76% of intended travelers chose domestic only and 48% would downgrade or shorten.

Japan’s inbound portfolio is diversifying under a China shock. Total H1 arrivals were 21.08M, down 2%, while Chinese arrivals fell 56.4%. Korea, Taiwan, Southeast Asia, Europe and the US offset much of the decline, and spend still reached a first-half record. Acquisition and host content need a more diversified source-market mix. Kyodo
Domestic travel is a yield story. Participation fell 0.4 percentage point to 48.9%, but total spend increased 2.5% to ¥8.3883T (~$56.7B), with on-site spend reaching 43.8% of the market. Distinctive stays should connect to food and activities, while total-price clarity remains essential. Travel Voice / Jalan
Japanese outbound stays short-haul and pre-planned. Korea and Taiwan lead intended destinations at 26% and 18%; 88% pre-book accommodation, but dining and transport are often chosen in-market. Airbnb must win the stay early and remain useful after booking through local discovery. Travel Voice / Visa

🇨🇳 China (中国)

• Late-August value opened quickly: Beijing–Urumqi fares fell 49.6% WoW and Beijing–Kunming 30.93%.

• Cooling-travel interest rose 180% MoM; three-night-plus stays made up 60% of homestay bookings.

• A 13-day Mid-Autumn/National Day leave pattern lifted domestic-city booking interest 30% YoY and outbound interest more than 50%.

Late summer creates an affordability window. Beijing–Urumqi round-trip fares fell from CNY3,750 (~$522) to CNY1,890 (~$263) in one week; five-day Beijing packages were about 20% below peak. Campaigns should focus on Aug 20 onward, cooling destinations and multi-night value. 中国网旅游
Regional inbound is scaling rapidly. European-origin orders rose 275% YoY, attraction-ticket orders more than twentyfold and Shenzhen one-day tours more than tenfold. Regional air arrivals rose 4x in Qinhuangdao, 3x in Erenhot and 2x in Luoyang. English-language neighborhood guidance and host readiness are becoming regional needs. Xinhua
Industrial tourism moves into the mainstream. Booking heat rose 36% YoY from Jul 1 to Aug 6; families were 68% of orders and inbound inquiries plus bookings rose more than ninefold. Factory, industrial-heritage and night-tour clusters create new family stay anchors outside classic sightseeing districts. China Daily

🌴 Southeast Asia (东南亚)

• Malaysia domestic spend grew 15.8%, more than twice visitor growth, while secondary-city Agoda searches accelerated up to 3.6x.

• Singapore is dispersing visitors through Grab vouchers and deepening its three-year Agoda data-and-AI partnership.

• Manila’s planned PHP5B (~$86M) cruise terminal could turn day-call traffic into pre- and post-cruise stays.

🇲🇾 Malaysia
Domestic travelers spend more and choose more flexibly. Q1 visitors rose 7.2% to 74.7M while spend rose 15.8% to RM34B (~$8.1B). Agoda searches for Kota Bharu increased 3.6x and Kuantan 2.8x ahead of Merdeka, making secondary-city supply and value-led short stays the immediate opportunity. The Rakyat Post
🇻🇳 Vietnam
Ho Chi Minh City prioritizes higher-value experience-led growth. The city is pushing night economy, culture, events, coastal and eco-tourism — a favorable direction for distinctive urban and longer-stay inventory. Exact publication timing was not independently verified, so the figures are treated as directional rather than headline KPIs. Viet Nam News
🇵🇭 Philippines · 🇸🇬 Singapore
Infrastructure and mobility partnerships shape distribution. Manila’s proposed terminal would handle four vessels and follows cruise traffic growth of about 50% in 2025. Singapore’s STB–Grab campaign uses 20%-off ride vouchers capped at S$5 (~$3.90) to direct SEA visitors into Katong–Joo Chiat, Tiong Bahru and late-night districts. Airbnb should pair flexible gateway stays with better post-booking mobility guidance. Philstar · TTG Asia

🇭🇰🇹🇼 Hong Kong & Taiwan

• Hong Kong H1 airport passenger traffic rose 11.7%, but projected mainland visitor yield remains low at about US$310 per visitor.

• Taiwan’s proposed weekday subsidy offers up to NT$2,000 (~$63), while current reporting suggests foreign OTA bookings may be excluded.

• European Taiwan searches are broadening beyond Taipei: Croatia +56%, Belgium +48%, with Kaohsiung and Taichung joining the top three cities.

🇭🇰 Hong Kong
Connectivity recovers faster than visitor yield. H1 airport passenger traffic increased 11.7%. Hong Kong and Macau are projected to capture nearly 40% of China outbound trips, but Hong Kong’s average spend is only about US$310 per visitor — favoring transit-convenient, short-stay and group-value positioning. Bloomberg · China Trading Desk
🇹🇼 Taiwan
Weekday incentives could redirect demand away from foreign platforms. The proposed pattern is NT$800 (~$25) off night one and NT$1,200 (~$38) off a consecutive second night, with 26 participating theme parks. Eligibility and final rules remain pending; Airbnb should not market subsidy access without confirmation. UDN Travel
Long-haul demand spreads beyond Taipei. Croatia lodging searches rose 56% YoY, Belgium 48% and the Czech Republic 16%; Taipei, Kaohsiung and Taichung were the most-searched cities. Multilingual content, rail-linked itineraries and longer-stay utility can convert this diversified interest. UDN Travel
