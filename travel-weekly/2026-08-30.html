🔥 Key Signals

  1. Experience is becoming the product. Food, heritage, trekking and medical journeys are pulling travellers beyond generic lodging.
  2. Governments are actively shaping dispersal. Korea’s subsidy, Taiwan’s repeat-visitor credits and Vietnam’s tourism plan all target higher-value, longer or more regional stays.
  3. Regional platforms keep building distribution muscle. The Trip.com–Jin Jiang alliance adds a local operating and fixed-commission model across four SEA markets.

💡 Airbnb APAC Implications

  1. Package stays around participation—not only destinations: food districts, trekking routes, medical extensions and culture-led events.
  2. Make regional and repeat travel easier to discover; policy is increasingly rewarding dispersal beyond capital-city gateways.
  3. Track SEA hotel distribution partnerships, while leaning into the whole-home and group occasions commodity hotel inventory cannot own.

  • Medical-tourist spending rose 66.5% YoY in July to ₩213.08B (~$157M). Source ↗
  • Korea is productising trekking with long-stay, regional itineraries for international hikers. Source ↗

What else moved

  2. The subsidy programme is designed to move demand toward rural and fishing areas—not merely discount urban weekends. Source ↗
  3. For Airbnb, regional itineraries and wellness extensions are a credible whole-home opportunity around Seoul-led trips. Source ↗
