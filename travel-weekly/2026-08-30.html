🇮🇳 India (भारत)

• Inbound season confidence is building around experiential, wellness and business demand; Bangladesh tourist visas have resumed. Source ↗

• Indian outbound operators are packaging experience-led autumn and winter itineraries across long-haul markets. Source ↗

• Singapore logged 600K+ Indian visitors in H1, reinforcing India’s importance for regional city breaks, MICE and cruises. Source ↗

What else moved
Thailand’s Mumbai roadshow is explicitly courting Indian weddings, MICE and sustainable trips. Source ↗
The pause in US immigrant-visa appointments may redirect some flexible demand toward Southeast Asia or domestic trips. Source ↗
Watch the fall booking curve: a more experience-led trip mix favours differentiated stays, not just hotel inventory. Source ↗

🇰🇷 Korea (대한민국)

• A domestic half-price travel programme expanded to nine more regions after ₩5.2B (~$3.8M) in refunds generated at least ₩16.2B (~$11.9M) in local spend. Source ↗

• Medical-tourist spending rose 66.5% YoY in July to ₩213.08B (~$157M). Source ↗

• Korea is productising trekking with long-stay, regional itineraries for international hikers. Source ↗

What else moved
Preventive health checkups are emerging as a Seoul inbound proposition: efficient diagnostics, lower waits and visitor services. Source ↗
The subsidy programme is designed to move demand toward rural and fishing areas—not merely discount urban weekends. Source ↗
For Airbnb, regional itineraries and wellness extensions are a credible whole-home opportunity around Seoul-led trips. Source ↗

🇯🇵 Japan (日本)

• July inbound arrivals reached a record 3.44M, up 0.1% YoY; Korea and Taiwan led growth while China remained weak. Source ↗

• Japanese outbound travel rose 13.3% YoY to 14.7M in 2025, but remains 26.6% below 2019. Source ↗

• Narita will restart its Transit & Stay programme on October 1, turning transfers into local experiences. Source ↗

What else moved
JTB and Bounce will offer same-day airport and long-distance baggage delivery, including Tokyo–Kyoto/Osaka. Source ↗
Uber Japan and Hinomaru Taxi will trial robotaxi operations in Tokyo late this year. Source ↗
The inbound mix is resilient but uneven: local trip utility and regional dispersion remain valuable complements to high-volume gateways. Source ↗

🇨🇳 China (中国)

• Food-led travel is accelerating: Douyin Life Services reported tea and fruit-juice orders +56% during summer. Source ↗

• Qingdao’s beer festival drew 7.18M visits, using old-town activations and technology to turn a festival into a city break. Source ↗

• Xi’an’s Tang-history talks and heritage crafts show the pull of participatory, culture-rich domestic travel. Source ↗

What else moved
Qingdao is bundling brewery visits, historic neighbourhoods and nightlife into a compact destination narrative. Source ↗
Yani Wetland links conservation and tourism livelihoods after ¥176M (~$24.5M) in protection and monitoring investment. Source ↗
The signal is “stay and participate”: food, local IP and culture can make the accommodation itself part of the itinerary. Source ↗

🇭🇰🇹🇼 Hong Kong & Taiwan
